Introduce the StudioMeyer Academy and get the reader moving.

1. Call `academy_welcome` on the `studiomeyer-academy` MCP server. Pass `locale` if `$ARGUMENTS` names one of `de`, `en`, `es`.
2. Call `academy_levels` for the six-level structure.
3. Say back, in the reader's language and in your own words: what the Academy is, what the six levels cover, and which one fits someone starting today.
4. Name one concrete first lesson and offer to open it with `/academy-lesson`.

Do not invent lessons, levels or recipe names. Everything comes from the two calls above.
