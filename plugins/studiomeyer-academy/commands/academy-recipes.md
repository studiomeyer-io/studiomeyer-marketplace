Work with the Academy recipes.

1. Without arguments: call `academy_recipes` and show what exists, grouped by phase, marking which are written and which are still placeholders.
2. With a slug: call `academy_recipe` and walk the reader through it step by step, pausing at each step that needs them to do something.
3. With a topic that is not a slug: call `academy_search` first, then open the best match.

Say plainly when a recipe is a placeholder rather than pretending it has content.
